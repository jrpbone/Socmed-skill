# Platform formats

Treat dimensions as production defaults, then verify current platform requirements when publishing.

| Platform | Format | Working size | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Pinterest | Standard pin | 1000 × 1500 | Vertical discovery; the idea must read before the caption |
| Facebook | Feed landscape | 1200 × 630 | Keep critical labels away from extreme edges |
| Threads | Feed portrait | 1080 × 1350 | Strong mobile crop and generous type size |
| X | Landscape | 1200 × 675 | One immediate idea; avoid dense body copy |
| LinkedIn | Landscape | 1200 × 627 | Evidence-led business visual |
| LinkedIn | Document page | 1200 × 1500 | One idea per page, consistent narrative progression |
| Instagram | Portrait | 1080 × 1350 | Mobile-first; direct labels and high contrast |
| Stories / Reels | Vertical | 1080 × 1920 | Respect interface safe zones |

## Adaptation rule

Do not resize a landscape composition into a portrait canvas. Preserve the takeaway and rebuild the spatial relationship:

- convergence may rotate from horizontal to vertical;
- concentric systems may stay centered but move labels into a vertical rail;
- layers may become a vertical cutaway;
- overlap may stay central while supporting copy moves below;
- screenshots may use a tighter crop with fewer callouts.
